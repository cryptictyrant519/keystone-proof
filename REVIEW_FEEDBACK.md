- report criticism even when no exploit or verification failure is found.

## Publication Rule

Feedback must be published accurately, including criticism.

Do not rewrite negative findings into endorsements.

For each review, record:

- reviewer name or public handle;
- relevant technical background, if supplied by the reviewer;
- review date;
- material reviewed;
- tests or attacks attempted;
- findings;
- criticism;
- Keystone response or remediation;
- status of any resulting issue;
- public source or link, when available.
